# Youth Sports Hub Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 17, 2026_

Youth Sports Hub puts your kids' games and practices on one family schedule. This policy says, in plain language, what we keep, why, who else sees it, and how to delete it.

**The short version:** accounts are for parents only. About your kids we keep a first name, a calendar color and their sports, nothing more. We don't show ads, we don't track you across other apps or websites, and we never sell or share your family's data.

## Who we are
Youth Sports Hub is an independent app. To reach us, send a message from the app (Profile › Help us improve › Send feedback) or use the contact details on our support page.

## What we keep and why

- **Your email address and a sign-in ID** from Apple, Google or email sign-in, to create your account and sign you in.
- **Your kids' first names, colors and sports**, to show whose event is whose.
- **The calendar links and team accounts you connect** (TeamSnap, GameChanger, SportsEngine, Sprocket Sports, league websites), to pull in your schedule and keep it up to date.
- **Games, practices, places, times and changes** from those sources, to show your schedule, flag what changed and spot conflicts.
- **Your RSVP answers**, to show what you've answered.
- **Short field tips you write**, shared with other parents who go to the same field.
- **Feedback and app requests you send us**, with the app and iOS version, to fix problems and decide what to build next.

We don't collect your contacts, photos, health data or browsing history, and we don't use analytics or advertising tools.

**Location:** the app uses your location only on your phone, to estimate drive time and "Leave by" times. It's never sent to us. Apple Maps receives it when you ask for directions or drive time, under Apple's privacy policy.

## Who else processes your data
We use a small number of service providers that act on our behalf and may use the data only to provide their service to us:

- **Supabase** stores your account and family data and runs our servers.
- **Anthropic** (the maker of Claude) powers **Ask**. When you use Ask, your question and your family's schedule (kids' first names, teams, times and places) are sent to Anthropic to produce the answer. The app asks your permission before the first time. Anthropic doesn't use this data to train its models.
- **TeamSnap**, only if you choose to connect it: we read your teams and schedule with the access you approve. We store the connection securely (encrypted) on our servers, never only on your phone.
- **Apple and Google**, if you sign in with them.

We don't sell your data, share it for advertising, or give it to data brokers. We would disclose data only if the law requires it.

## Kids
Youth Sports Hub is for parents and other adults. Kids never get accounts. We keep only a first name, a color and sports for each kid: no last names, birthdays, ages or photos. We don't knowingly collect information directly from children.

## Shared field tips
Tips you write about a field (parking, which entrance) are shown to other parents, without your name. Don't include anyone's name in a tip. Every tip can be reported or hidden.

## Keeping and deleting your data
- We keep your data while you have an account.
- **Delete your account** in the app at Profile › Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything in it: kids, sources and connections, events, RSVPs, feedback and your tips. Server logs, which hold no schedule details, are deleted after 7 days.
- **Your subscribable calendar link** can be reset anytime in Profile, which turns off the old link.
- To get a copy of your data, or with any question about it, send us a message from the app or use our support page.

## Security
Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Connected account tokens are encrypted on our servers. Each family can only ever see its own data.

## Changes
If we change this policy we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change matters.
