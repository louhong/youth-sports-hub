## Common questions

### How do I add my kids' teams?
Go to **Home › + Add a source** (or the "Connect sources" step when you first sign up) and pick the app your team uses:

- **TeamSnap:** tap **Connect** and sign in to TeamSnap. Youth Sports Hub can read your schedule and RSVP status; it can't change anything in TeamSnap.
- **GameChanger:** in GameChanger, open the team, tap the gear icon › **Schedule Sync**, copy the link, and paste it into Youth Sports Hub.
- **SportsEngine:** copy the team's calendar (iCal) link from SportsEngine and paste it in.
- **Sprocket Sports:** on the team's schedule, tap **Subscribe › Copy to Clipboard**, then paste the link. Add one link per team.
- **Any other app or league website:** if it offers a calendar link (it often starts with `webcal://` or ends in `.ics`), paste it under **Other app or league website**.

New events and changes usually show up within 15 minutes.

### Some games or practices are missing
What a calendar link includes depends on your role in the team's app. For example, GameChanger "followers" only get games, not practices. Ask the team manager to add you as a parent or guardian, then copy the link again.

### How do I RSVP?
Tap **RSVP in [app]** on an event. It opens the team's app so your answer reaches the coach. Many team apps don't allow outside apps to send RSVPs.

### Can I see the schedule in Apple, Google or Outlook Calendar?
Yes. Go to **Profile › Add to your calendar**. Apple Calendar subscribes in one tap; for Google or Outlook, copy the link and add it as a calendar "from URL". If you ever share the link by mistake, tap **Reset link** to turn the old one off.

### What is Ask?
Ask answers questions about your family's schedule, like "When's the next game?" It uses Claude, an AI model from Anthropic, and asks your permission before the first question. Ask can't RSVP, send messages or change anything. You can turn it off in **Profile › Let Ask use AI**.

### How do I delete my account?
Go to **Profile › Delete account**. This permanently deletes your account and everything in it: kids, connected sources, schedules and any tips you've shared.

### Is my family's data private?
Yes. We keep only a first name, a color and sports for each kid, we don't show ads, and we never sell or share your data. Read the full [privacy policy](privacy.html).
